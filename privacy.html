Tebga – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Tebga does not collect any personal data.

What that means

  • There are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • There are no ads.
  • There is no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • The game works offline. It does not send anything about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (language, round time, sound and vibration) and which words you have already played are saved on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The one-time "Unlock everything" purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your card or your Apple ID. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

Tebga is a family game. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.

Support and contact

Questions or problems? Email us at tebga@proton.me.

ئەوەی لەسەر مۆبایلەکەت دەمێنێتەوە

کڕین

منداڵان

تێبگە یارییەکی خێزانییە. لەبەر ئەوەی هیچ زانیارییەک لە هیچ کەسێک کۆ ناکاتەوە، لە منداڵانیش کۆی ناکاتەوە.

ئەگەر وەشانێکی داهاتوو هەر یەکێک لەمانە بگۆڕێت، سەرەتا ئەم پەڕەیە نوێ دەکرێتەوە و بەروارەکەی سەرەوە دەگۆڕێت.
